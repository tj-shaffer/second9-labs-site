Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and the things we will never do with it. We don't sell your data — ever.

Last updated · June 15, 2026

This policy explains how Second 9 Labs, LLC ("Second 9 Labs," "we," "us") handles the information of people who use our website and our applications, including Third Brain. We've kept it brief on purpose.

What we collect

Only what's needed to create your account and run the service:

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your mobile phone number, if you choose to receive text messages.
  • The content and settings you create inside Third Brain.

How we use it

We use your information solely to provide and operate the service — to sign you in, save your work, and send account and service messages you ask for. That's it.

What we never do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Ever.

We do not share your information with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. We don't build advertising profiles, and we don't hand your data to data brokers.

Text messaging

If you provide your mobile number and opt in, we may send you account-related and service text messages (for example, sign-in codes or important notices). Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe, or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

Service providers

We rely on a small number of trusted providers to operate the service — for example, infrastructure hosting and the messaging carrier that delivers our texts. They may process your information only as needed to perform that work on our behalf, and they are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time, and we'll do it.

Your choices

You can access, correct, or delete your information, opt out of text messages, or close your account by contacting us. We'll respond promptly.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email admin@secondninelabs.com. Second 9 Labs, LLC is a small company based in the United States.

We may update this policy as the service evolves. When we do, we'll revise the date at the top of this page.
