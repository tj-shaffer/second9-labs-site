Vault

Store grocery-service credentials so the assistant can build your cart for you. Credentials are AES-GCM encrypted with a per-user key derived from a server master secret. The agent stops one tap short of checkout — you always confirm.

Sign in to use the vault. Sign in →
Read this before saving credentials:
  • Instacart's Terms of Service prohibit automated access. Using this could lead Instacart to suspend or ban your account.
  • The agent never confirms checkout. You always tap "Place Order" yourself.
  • Credentials are encrypted at rest, used only at agent-run time, never logged.
  • You can delete credentials any time. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.
  • Every agent action is logged for transparency (see Settings → Agent log).
